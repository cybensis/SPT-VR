SPT-VR · VR for Single Player Tarkov

Controls

Most of SPT-VR is done with your hands: you reach for things on your body and on your gear and grab them. The buttons do the rest. Pick a situation below to see what every button does in it.

Gun hand your right hand, the one holding the gun Off hand your left hand, for the foregrip, gear and loot Either hand can take a gun. Grab one with the left and it becomes the gun hand.

Buttons by situation

Buttons shown on a Quest-style layout: A and B on the right controller, X and Y on the left. Lit buttons do something in the situation you picked.

Your body

Your guns, grenades and gear sit on your body. Reach for one and use the grip or trigger there. A short buzz tells you that your hand is in place.

Seen from behind, so your left is on the left. The dashed ring is on your front.

  • Head

    Left grip: flip your visor, face shield or night vision. Left trigger: headlight on or off.

  • Behind your left shoulder

    Hold the left grip and bring your hand in front of you: the quick-slot menu. Pick with the stick, let go to use it. Left trigger: drop your backpack. Let go of an item here (either hand) to put it in your inventory.

  • Behind your right shoulder

    Your second primary. Grip it to take it.

  • Chest

    Your gun hangs across your chest when you let go of it. Grip it by its grip to take it back. The grenade slot is at the top left: grip it with either hand.

  • Right hip

    Your pistol. Grip it to draw it.

  • Knife

    On its sheath, wherever your gear carries it. Grip it to draw it, then swing it.

  • Left wrist

    Look at it for your health, stance, exit timers and notifications.

  • Move a spot

    Hold the trigger on a gun or the grenade at its spot for a second. It comes off and follows your hand: let go of the trigger where you want it.

    VR tab › Weapons › Reset Placed Gun Spots puts them all back.

How things work

The parts that are more than one button press.

Aiming and scopes

  • Raise the sight to your eye to aim. There is no aim button. Lower the gun or look away to stop.
  • While aiming, hold the left trigger to hold your breath. The gun steadies until your lungs run out.
  • Bring a gun with a stock up to your shoulder and it steadies.
  • Zoom: while aiming a zoom scope, push the right stick up or down. Or put your left hand on the scope, hold the grip and twist.
  • Left hand on a red dot, holo or scope, tap the grip: switches its mode or brightness.

Holding and switching guns

  • The gun stays in your hand while you hold the grip. Open your hand and it hangs across your chest.
  • Let go with your gun hand while the left holds the foregrip, and the left hand keeps the gun. It can't fire like that.
  • Take another gun straight off your body: second primary behind your right shoulder, pistol on your right hip.
  • Pass a rifle to your other hand: put that hand on the gun hand's grip and press its grip. A pistol: hold it with both hands and let go with the gun hand.
  • With Toggle Gun Grip on, press the grip to take hold and press again to let go.

Working the gun

  • Reach your left hand to a part of the gun (the magazine, the bolt, the selector, the stock): its menu appears. The right stick picks, the left trigger or A does it. Check chamber, check magazine, inspect, fire mode, fold stock.
  • The part menu doesn't open while you aim or hold the foregrip.
  • Fire mode: A while holding the foregrip.
  • Light or laser: left hand on the foregrip, tap Y. Hold Y to switch its mode.
  • Under-barrel grenade launcher: hold the left trigger and press X. Again to switch back.
  • Press check on a pistol (Index and Quest controllers): let go of the grip with your thumb resting on a button or the stick and your finger resting on the trigger. Your hand goes onto the slide. Squeeze the trigger to draw it back.

Loot, containers and bodies

  • Point an open palm at loose items: dots mark what you can grab. The grip pulls it into your hand.
  • Let go to drop it, or swing and let go to throw it. Let go behind your left shoulder to put it in your inventory.
  • Either hand works while it isn't holding the gun.
  • Containers: put your hand in and pull the trigger.
  • Bodies: palm on the body, pull the trigger for its loot. Hold the grip to drag it.
  • Look at anything you can use to get its menu. The right stick picks, A selects.

Doors and keys

  • Grip the handle, turn it, then push or pull the door. Let go while it moves and it keeps swinging.
  • A locked door rattles and won't open.
  • Unlock: hold the locked door's handle and pull the trigger. Your key comes into your hand. Put its tip in the lock and twist your wrist. Pull the trigger again to put the key away.
  • Breach: hit a shut door hard with your gun, in the direction it opens. Kicking and keycards go through the look-at menu.

Grenades

  • The grenade you'd throw next sits at the top left of your chest. Grip it with either hand and keep holding.
  • With your other hand, pull the trigger on the ring and pull the pin out. Grenades without a ring are ready at once.
  • Swing and let go to throw. Let go before the pin is out and it goes back on your chest.
  • Changed your mind: bring the pin back to the grenade and push it in.
  • Before pulling the pin, hold the trigger of the hand holding it to pick a different grenade with the stick.
  • Hold A for tripwire mode, then the trigger places its posts.

Mounted guns

  • With both hands free (empty, or your gun slung), grip both handles. The Utyos only needs the right one.
  • Turn it with your hands and fire with the trigger. Put your eye to the sight to aim.
  • Open both hands to get off.

Eating and drinking

  • Use food from your inventory or a quick slot. It comes out in your hands.
  • Open it with your free hand: put it on the lid, cap or wrapper, squeeze the trigger, then pull or tilt.
  • Bring it to your mouth to eat. Hold a drink at your mouth to drink and lower it to stop. Trigger at the cap puts it back on.
  • Some foods want a shake over your other palm or a reach into the bag.
  • Both triggers at once: put it away.

Setup and comfort

  • Reset your height: stand straight, look ahead and hold the right stick click for half a second.
  • Do it with your arms straight out to the sides and it also measures your arm length.
  • Crouch, lean and peek with your body. Your character follows you.
  • Playing seated: turn on Seated Mode, then reset your height.
  • Valve Index: the same buttons. Touching the left trackpad also sprints, grabs need a squeeze, and your fingers are tracked.
  • Vive wands: click the top half of the right trackpad to jump (hold to vault). Hold your thumb low on it to crouch. Both can be tuned on the VR tab under Movement.

Not in this guide: reloading

Magazines, charging handles, bolts, pumps, break-actions, cylinders and clearing jams are all done by hand, the way you would with the real gun. Figuring that out is part of the game. Every gun works a little differently.
